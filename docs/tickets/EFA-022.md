# EFA-022 — Compare two clips around explicit synchronization anchors

**Status:** planned
**Milestone:** M2 — Faster film study and deeper Kinovea interoperability
**Priority:** P1
**Area:** playback
**GitHub issue:** [#22](https://github.com/mfreeze77/exais-football-ai/issues/22)

## Outcome and user value

Compare repetitions or two camera angles at the moment that matters, such as the snap.

## Scope

- Create comparison records referencing two clips and source-relative anchor timestamps.
- Provide synchronized play/pause, adjustable offset, independent zoom, and a clear behavior at interval boundaries.

## Non-goals

- Automatic temporal alignment, geometric camera fusion, or claiming calibrated measurements from arbitrary zoom.

## Acceptance checks

- [ ] Two generated videos with known offsets align within a declared playback tolerance.
- [ ] Changing a comparison offset does not rewrite either source or clip trim.
- [ ] Each panel identifies its game/clip and whether it shows original or derived media.
- [ ] Pause/seek/speed controls behave consistently when one clip reaches its boundary.
- [ ] Saved comparisons reopen with their selected clips, anchors, and layout.
