# EFA-039 — Detect and review tracking gaps and identity switches

**Status:** planned
**Milestone:** M3 — Qualified optional football computer vision
**Priority:** P2
**Area:** analysis
**GitHub issue:** [#39](https://github.com/mfreeze77/exais-football-ai/issues/39)

## Outcome and user value

Prevent a convincing overlay from following the wrong player after a collision or occlusion.

## Scope

- Add track-quality flags for impossible jumps, long gaps, fragmented paths, edge exits, and conflicting overlaps.
- Provide reviewed suppress/split/link suggestions with provenance and invalidate affected downstream views.

## Non-goals

- Claiming perfect multi-object tracking, automatic cross-camera identity, or modifying provider artifacts in place.

## Acceptance checks

- [ ] Synthetic track fixtures expose known gaps and swaps with reproducible diagnostics.
- [ ] A manual split/link decision is scoped to source/run/time and preserves original tracks.
- [ ] Changing track continuity marks dependent position/identity/render decisions stale until reviewed.
- [ ] Low-quality tracks remain visible as uncertain or can be hidden without pretending they were never detected.
- [ ] The real-film evaluation records examples where stitching improves or harms results.
