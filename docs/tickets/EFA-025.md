# EFA-025 — Add focused playlist review sessions and progress

**Status:** planned
**Milestone:** M2 — Faster film study and deeper Kinovea interoperability
**Priority:** P1
**Area:** playlists
**GitHub issue:** [#25](https://github.com/mfreeze77/exais-football-ai/issues/25)

## Outcome and user value

Pick up a film session where the owner stopped and revisit the most useful plays.

## Scope

- Persist a local session with playlist snapshot, clip position, replay count, and personal watch-again markers.
- Provide next/previous, loop-current, review notes, and explicit finish/reset controls.

## Non-goals

- Multi-user attendance analytics, notifications to other people, or grading players automatically.

## Acceptance checks

- [ ] A stopped session resumes at its stored clip and source-relative time after restart.
- [ ] A subsequent playlist edit does not silently change a session's frozen order.
- [ ] Replay counts and watch-again markers are local viewing metadata and do not change coaching approval.
- [ ] The owner can reset progress without deleting clip notes or video.
- [ ] Archived/missing references produce a recoverable state that preserves remaining order.
